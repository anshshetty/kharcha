# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
One person using Kharcha on their Mac, primarily with INR transactions. Their first job is to understand monthly spending, then drill into and correct payments.

## Product Purpose
A local-first personal spending tracker importing Gmail transaction evidence and bank statements into a persistent local ledger.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve shared expenses, refunds, transfers, evidence review, merchant identification, manual transactions, exports, encrypted backups, and explicit optional AI consent. Never combine currencies or invent exchange rates. Viewing a new payment is distinct from resolving an evidence issue. Confirmed identities and merchant descriptors support category rules; bank email senders do not identify payees.

## Brand Commitments
Kharcha name and existing icon. Precise compact product UI, neutral surfaces, charcoal text, forest green selections, informative financial graphics, a custom receipt illustration, light and dark themes.

## Product Principles
- Put monthly understanding before configuration.
- Corrections save automatically and future matching scope is visible where the category changes.
- Expose advanced accounting only when relevant.
- Every total can lead to its supporting payments.

## Evidence on Hand
Synthetic disposable demo in scripts/demo.py and docs/demo.md. No personal ledger is used for captures or tests.
